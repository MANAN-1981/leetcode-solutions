## Common Follow-up Questions

1. Can you explain the time complexity of the algorithm?
2. How does the algorithm handle the case where the matrix is empty?
3. What are some potential edge cases to consider?

## Strengths

- The solution is straightforward and easy to understand.
- The solution has a low time complexity.
- The solution is well-organized and easy to follow.

## Weaknesses

- The solution does not handle the case where the matrix is empty.
