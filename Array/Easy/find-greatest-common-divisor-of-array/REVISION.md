## Key Idea

The key insight is that the GCD of an array of numbers is the same as the GCD of the smallest and largest numbers in the array. This is because the GCD of a set of numbers is the largest number that divides all numbers in the set without leaving a remainder.

## Important Observations

Important observations include: the GCD of two numbers a and b is the same as the GCD of b and the remainder of a divided by b, the GCD of an array of numbers is the same as the GCD of the smallest and largest numbers in the array.
