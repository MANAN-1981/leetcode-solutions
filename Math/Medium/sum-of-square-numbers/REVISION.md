# Quick Revision

## Pattern Used

TwoPointers

## Key Idea

The key insight is to use a two-pointer approach to systematically check all possible pairs of square numbers that could sum up to the target number. The algorithm's efficiency relies on the fact that the sum of two square numbers is monotonically increasing as the pointers move.

## Important Observations

Critical observations include: the target number must be non-negative, the square numbers must be non-negative, and the algorithm's time complexity is O(sqrt(c))
