## Key Idea

The key insight is to move the pointer that points to the shorter container towards the other pointer, ensuring that all possible combinations of containers are considered. This approach allows us to find the maximum area in a single pass through the array.

## Important Observations

Critical observations include: the area of water that can be trapped between two containers is determined by the height of the shorter container and the distance between them, the maximum area is found by considering all possible combinations of containers.
