## Common Follow-up Questions

1. Can you explain the mathematical basis for the formulas used to calculate the sums?
2. How does the algorithm handle edge cases?
3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using a recursive function?

---

## Strengths

- The solution is well-organized and easy to follow.
- The code is concise and efficient.
- The algorithm is based on a proven mathematical concept.

---

## Weaknesses

- The solution may not be optimized for large values of n.
- The recursive function may cause a stack overflow for very large values of n.

---

## How To Improve

- Optimize the calculation of the sums using mathematical formulas.
- Use an iterative function instead of a recursive function.
- Add error handling for edge cases.

---

## Would This Pass a FAANG Interview?

Yes, the solution is well-organized and efficient, and the algorithm is based on a proven mathematical concept. However, the solution may need to be optimized for large values of n and error handling may need to be added for edge cases.
