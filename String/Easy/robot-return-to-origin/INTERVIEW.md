## Common Follow-up Questions

1. Can you explain the algorithm's time and space complexity?
2. How does the algorithm handle edge cases?
3. What are some potential optimizations for the algorithm?

---

## Strengths

- The algorithm is simple and efficient.
- The algorithm accurately simulates the robot's movements.
- The algorithm's time and space complexity are well-balanced.

---

## Weaknesses

- The algorithm may not be suitable for very large inputs.
- The algorithm does not handle non-grid movements.

---

## How To Improve

- Consider optimizing the algorithm for large inputs.
- Consider adding error handling for invalid inputs.
- Consider exploring alternative approaches to solving the problem.

## Would This Pass a FAANG Interview?
