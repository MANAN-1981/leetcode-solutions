## Pattern Used

Array

## Key Idea

The key insight is to use two separate arrays to store the minimum of each row and the maximum of each column, and then check for common elements between the two arrays. This allows us to efficiently find the lucky numbers in the matrix.

## Important Observations

Important observations include the fact that the minimum of each row and the maximum of each column can be found in a single pass over the matrix, and that the lucky numbers are the numbers that are both the minimum of their row and the maximum of their column.
