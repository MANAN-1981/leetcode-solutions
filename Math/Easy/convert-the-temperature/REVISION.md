## Key Idea

The key insight is that the conversions can be performed using simple arithmetic formulas. The formulas are based on the definitions of the temperature scales and are widely accepted.

## Important Observations

Important observations include: the conversions are linear, the formulas are based on the definitions of the temperature scales, and the algorithm does not involve any loops or recursive calls.

## Formula

Celsius to Kelvin: K = C + 273.15, Celsius to Fahrenheit: F = C * 1.80 + 32.00

## Interview Tips

When explaining this problem in an interview, be sure to emphasize the simplicity and efficiency of the algorithm. Also, be prepared to discuss the edge cases and how they are handled.
