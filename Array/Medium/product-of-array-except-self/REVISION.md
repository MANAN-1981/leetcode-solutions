# Quick Revision

## Pattern Used

PrefixSum

## Key Idea

The key insight is to calculate the prefix and suffix products independently and then multiply them to get the product of all other numbers for each element. This approach allows us to avoid dividing by zero and handling edge cases more efficiently.

## Important Observations

Critical observations include the fact that the prefix and suffix products can be calculated in linear time, and that the product of all other numbers for each element can be calculated by multiplying the corresponding prefix and suffix products.
