The key idea is to use the built-in Math.pow function to calculate the power of x and n. Alternatively, a recursive or iterative method can be used to calculate the power.

## Important Observations

Important observations include: the property of exponentiation that states x^n = (x^(n/2))^2 when n is even, and the fact that x^(-n) = 1/x^n.
