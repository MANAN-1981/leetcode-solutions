## Common Follow-up Questions

1. Can you explain the time and space complexity of the solution?
2. How does the algorithm handle arrays with different lengths?
3. What are some alternative approaches to solving this problem?

---

## Strengths

- The solution is efficient and easy to understand.
- The solution takes advantage of the discount to minimize the total cost.
- The solution handles edge cases properly.

---

## Weaknesses

- The solution may not be suitable for very large arrays due to the sorting step.
- The solution does not handle arrays with duplicate elements in a special way.

---

## How To Improve

- Consider using a more efficient sorting algorithm.
- Add comments to the code to explain the logic.
- Consider handling arrays with duplicate elements in a special way.

## Would This Pass a FAANG Interview?

Yes, the solution is efficient, easy to understand, and handles edge cases properly. However, the interviewer may ask follow-up questions to test the candidate's understanding of the solution and their ability to optimize it.
