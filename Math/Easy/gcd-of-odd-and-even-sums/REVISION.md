## Key Idea

The key idea is to use the Euclidean algorithm to calculate the GCD of the sums of odd and even numbers. This algorithm is based on the principle that the GCD of two numbers does not change if the larger number is replaced by its difference with the smaller number.

## Important Observations

Important observations include that the sum of the first n odd numbers is n^2, and that the sum of the first n even numbers is n(n+1). These observations can be used to optimize the calculation of the sums.

## Formula

GCD(a, b) = GCD(b, a mod b)
