## Key Idea

The key insight is to use a two-pointer approach to iterate through the array from both ends. This allows for efficient computation of the alternating sum.

## Important Observations

Critical observations include: the array is iterated through from both ends, elements at even indices are added and elements at odd indices are subtracted, and the pointers move towards each other until they meet or cross.
